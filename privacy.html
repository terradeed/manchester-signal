Who we are

Manchester Signal is a free weekly email newsletter published by Terradeed Ltd, trading as TerraDeed Labs ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it.
  • Signup details: the date and time you signed up and confirmed, the form you used, and the wording you agreed to. We keep this as a record of your consent.
  • Delivery data: whether an email was delivered, bounced or marked as spam, and whether you unsubscribed.
  • No open or click tracking: we have turned off open tracking and click tracking in our email provider, Kit. Our emails do not contain a tracking pixel, links in our emails go straight to the page they name, and we do not record whether you opened an email or which links you clicked.
  • No extra link tags: we do not add your subscriber ID, your email address or analytics tags to links.
  • Anything you send us, for example if you reply to an issue or email us a correction.

We do not buy, rent or scrape email lists. We only email people who signed up and confirmed their address themselves.

What you agree to when you sign up

This is the exact wording shown under our signup form:

By signing up you agree to receive the Manchester Signal newsletter by email from Terradeed Ltd, trading as TerraDeed Labs. We will send you an email to confirm first. We use Kit to send the newsletter. We do not track whether you open our emails or which links you click. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. Read our privacy notice.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • Sending you the newsletter. Lawful basis: consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Under PECR we only send marketing emails with your consent.
  • Keeping a record of your consent and honouring unsubscribes. Lawful basis: legal obligation and legitimate interests (being able to show you consented, and making sure we do not email you after you leave).
  • Replying to messages you send us. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (answering you).

You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking unsubscribe in any email, or by emailing a.gentry@terradeed.co.uk. Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before.

Who we share it with

  • Kit (kit.com), our email provider, which stores the list and sends the newsletter on our behalf as a data processor. Kit is based in the United States. We transfer your details to Kit under the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, which forms part of Kit's data processing agreement (kit.com/dpa, checked 23 Oct 2026).
  • Our website host, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may log technical data such as IP addresses when you visit manchestersignal.co.uk. [CHECK: confirm GitHub's logging and transfer terms before going live.]
  • Sponsors never receive your personal data. If a sponsor appears in an issue, we only share combined figures, such as the total number of subscribers.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • While you are subscribed, we keep your details.
  • When you unsubscribe, Kit stops all emails to you straight away. Your record stays in our Kit account marked as unsubscribed, so we have proof of your original consent and can make sure you are not emailed again. We delete unsubscribed records after 24 months. If you would like your details deleted sooner, email a.gentry@terradeed.co.uk and we will have them deleted, including from Kit's backups, within one month.
  • Checking you still want it: because we do not track opens or clicks, we cannot tell who reads the emails. Every 24 months we will email you to ask whether you still want Manchester Signal, and we will unsubscribe you if you do not confirm.
  • Kit's own records: Kit says it keeps email statistics, such as sends, bounces and unsubscribes, for 5 years on a rolling basis, after which they are archived rather than deleted (Kit: email data retention, checked 22 Oct 2026). If your subscriber record is deleted, Kit removes it together with its email history (Kit: deleting vs unsubscribing, checked 23 Oct 2026).
  • Correspondence: up to 2 years after our last exchange. [CHECK: proposed period.]

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access)
  • correct it (rectification)
  • delete it (erasure)
  • limit how we use it (restriction)
  • stop using it (objection)
  • send it to you or another organisation in a usable format (portability)

You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email a.gentry@terradeed.co.uk. We will reply within one month. There is no charge in most cases.

Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell subscribers in the newsletter.
