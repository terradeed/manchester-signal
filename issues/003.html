Liverpool City Region gets the government's AI engineers, with the data rules agreed first

On Friday 20 November, AI Minister Kanishka Narayan and Mayor Steve Rotheram agreed a 12-month partnership between the government's Incubator for AI (i.AI) and Liverpool City Region Combined Authority. The aim is to build AI tools that help 16 to 24-year-olds into jobs, apprenticeships and training, starting with young people not in education, employment or training, or at risk of it. Liverpool City Region is the first region to sign a memorandum of understanding with i.AI. Work begins in January 2027, with i.AI engineers working day to day inside the Combined Authority and its six councils. (GOV.UK, 20 Nov 2026)

Two details stand out. The partners say they will put data-sharing agreements and privacy assessments in place before accessing or using personal information, and that "trained professionals will always make the decisions about a young person's support". The work will also follow the Liverpool City Region Community Charter on Data and AI, written by residents. (GOV.UK, 20 Nov 2026)

The same announcement says Kyndryl is supporting the Skills Builder Partnership to create resources for schools and colleges in the city region on how skills such as teamwork and problem solving fit alongside AI, with the aim of reaching 25,000 young people. (GOV.UK, 20 Nov 2026)

Why it matters to you: if you hire school leavers or apprentices on Merseyside, the advisers who send young people your way may be using these tools from 2027. And the order of work is worth copying in any firm: agree what data can be used first, add AI second, and keep a named person making the final call. If customer or staff details are involved, the ICO's guidance for small organisations is the place to start. (ICO)

What's changing locally

Company directors: the identity check transition period is over. Identity verification for Companies House became a legal requirement on 18 November 2025, and on 19 November 2026 Companies House updated its guidance "for the end of the 12 month identity verification transition period". The guidance says that if you continue to act as a director without completing all verification requirements, you will be committing an offence, and the company and all its directors may be too. Verifying through GOV.UK One Login is free, by app, by answering questions online, or at a participating Post Office. You can also use an authorised agent such as your accountant, who may charge. (GOV.UK, Verifying your identity for Companies House, updated 19 Nov 2026; Companies House approach to non-compliance, updated 19 Nov 2026)

Why it matters to you: this covers every UK limited company, not just the North West. Check that each director and each person with significant control has a Companies House personal code linked to their role. If anyone hasn't, sort it before Companies House writes to you.

Why it matters to you: if you have one AI idea you keep meaning to test, this is four days of structured help at no charge, with a date in the diary.

One thing worth doing: put a spending limit on AI tools that bill by use

The job: finding every AI tool your business pays for by usage (credits, tokens, tasks or "consumption"), and capping it.

Why this week: at Microsoft Ignite on 17 November, Microsoft said its Copilot commercial model has two parts: a per-user subscription, and "a usage-based consumption model" for the hardest tasks. It also said that in Copilot Cowork, "your team sets a spending limit, so there's no runaway spend and no surprises when the bill arrives." (Microsoft Copilot blog, 17 Nov 2026)

Try it:

  1. Open the billing page (not the marketing page) of each AI tool you pay for and note whether any part is charged by use.
  2. For each one that is, set a monthly limit or an alert at an amount you'd be comfortable spending without a second look. If the tool has no limit, set an alert on the card or account it bills.
  3. Name one person who can switch on paid add-ons or raise a limit.

When not to bother:

  • If every AI tool you use is a flat monthly fee or a free plan, skip this.
  • If you don't use Microsoft 365, the Copilot news doesn't apply, but the check still does for any tool that bills by use.
